Buyers now ask ChatGPT "what's the best CRM for a small agency?" and take the answer. If your brand isn't in it, you never get the click. AI visibility tools (also called GEO or AEO tools) show you where you stand and why.

## What an AI visibility tool should tell you

1. **Mentions.** Is your brand named in the answer, and where in the list?
2. **Citations.** Which pages did the model cite? Yours, a competitor's, or a review site?
3. **Searches.** What did the model actually search for before answering?
4. **Model coverage.** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity give different answers. One model isn't enough.
5. **Change over time.** Answers shift every month as models and the web change.

## How to improve your AI visibility

1. **Run your buyers' real questions** through every major model, not just ChatGPT.
2. **Look at what they searched.** Those queries are your new keyword list.
3. **Study the cited pages.** Match their structure: a clear list, a comparison table, "best for" labels, an FAQ and a visible updated date.
4. **Get onto the pages that get cited.** If every model cites the same three listicles, getting added there beats writing a tenth one.
5. **Re-check monthly.** Answers change as models update.

## FAQ

### What is an AI visibility tool?
It's software that checks whether AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity mention your brand when people ask about your category, and which sources they cite.

### What is the difference between GEO and AEO?
They mean nearly the same thing. GEO (generative engine optimization) and AEO (answer engine optimization) both mean getting your brand named and cited in AI-generated answers.

### How do I check if ChatGPT recommends my business?
Ask it the questions your buyers ask, ideally through a tool like LLM Sleuth that runs the same question across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity and shows the searches and citations behind each answer.

### Do AI visibility tools work for local businesses and agencies?
Yes. Agencies use them to show clients where they stand in AI answers, then plan content and listings to close the gap.
